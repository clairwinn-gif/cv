Xiaowen (Winn) Ge

Researcher in AI & XR Applications for Music Education

Edinburgh, UK  ·  winn_g@outlook.com  ·  ORCID: 0009-0004-3830-9481

Profile

PhD in Education (University of Glasgow, 2024), qualitative research design specialist (interviews, video-recorded observation, thematic analysis). Current work moves into AI/XR music-education contexts: designing Syn-Aesthetica (a Python/C++ system for music-feature analysis and emotion classification, intended to drive physical/kinetic output) and teaching NVivo-based qualitative methods to VR/generative-AI pedagogy postgraduates at Liverpool. The next step is applying that same qualitative approach to how existing AI/XR tools are actually used, understood and negotiated by learners and teachers — evaluation and methodology, not tool-building for its own sake.

Research Focus

  • Creativity in education — core PhD strand applying Csikszentmihalyi's Domain-Field-Individual model — the Field as institutional gatekeeper selecting which novelty is transmitted — to how teachers mediate three curriculum-defined creativity dimensions (sound exploration, improvisation, composition); related peer-reviewed journal article forthcoming (Music Education Research).
  • Cross-stage teacher mediation of musical engagement — collaborative secondary analysis combining two independent PhD datasets (with Dr Sicong Li and Prof. Oscar Odena) comparing nursery and primary music education in China; manuscript in development, target: Australasian Journal of Early Childhood.

Related projects

  • Syn-Aesthetica (2025–present, with jewellery-design PhD collaborator Dong Ding, Edinburgh College of Art) — Python/C++ tool intended to combine librosa feature extraction with a self-trained lightweight emotion classifier (public affective-music datasets) to drive physical/kinetic output via Ding's sculptural mechanisms. Role: project strategy and funding-proposal writing, analysis pipeline, classifier training, and the educational-evaluation framework; prototype/demo stage. Two outputs in preparation: a scoping review positioning the field (Ding first author; target: CoDesign) and a software paper (Ge first author; target: JOSS).
  • XR research design for inclusive pedagogy — authored a full proposal for the ESRC North West Social Science DTP (NWSSDTP) Postdoctoral Fellowship (October 2025 entry) on XR-based music pedagogy for neurodivergent learners, drawing on AR/holographic display technology from Liverpool's Design4Life lab (Dr Hao-Ming Xia's museum and cultural-heritage AR research) — not taken forward at the institutional nomination stage; developed a participatory co-design and cognitive-science evaluation framework now informing the evaluation design being developed for Syn-Aesthetica.

Education

01/2019–06/2024 PhD in Education — University of Glasgow

Thesis: Music teachers' perceptions of creativity in the context of twenty-first century Chinese education (viva 27 September 2023). Supervisors: Prof. Oscar Odena, Prof. Kay Livingston.

Qualitative design: 17 semi-structured interviews, 24 lesson observations, critical-incident charting, researcher diary; thematic analysis in NVivo. Produced an original seven-stage model of creativity development in music education. Key finding: conservatoire-trained instrumentalists received formal improvisation training; generalist teachers engaged with it only informally.
