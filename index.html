I'm a PhD in Education (University of Glasgow, 2024), and a qualitative researcher at heart — interviews, video-recorded observation, thematic analysis. These days that same lens is turning toward AI and XR in music education: I'm designing Syn-Aesthetica, a tool intended to turn music analysis into physical, kinetic movement, and teaching qualitative methods to VR and generative-AI pedagogy postgraduates at Liverpool.

The through-line: I study how tools are actually used, understood and negotiated by the people learning and teaching with them — evaluation and methodology, grounded in real classroom practice.

Research focus

Creativity in education

Core PhD strand developing an original theoretical framework and model, building on Csikszentmihalyi's Domain-Field-Individual model (the Field as institutional gatekeeper selecting which novelty is transmitted) among other creativity theories, to explain how teachers mediate three curriculum-defined creativity dimensions (sound exploration, improvisation, composition). Related peer-reviewed journal article forthcoming (Music Education Research).

Related projects

Syn-Aesthetica — 2025–present

With jewellery-design PhD collaborator Dong Ding (Edinburgh College of Art). A Python/C++ tool intended to combine librosa feature extraction with a self-trained lightweight emotion classifier (public affective-music datasets) to drive physical/kinetic output via Ding's sculptural mechanisms. My role: project strategy and funding-proposal writing, the analysis pipeline, classifier training, and the educational-evaluation framework — currently at prototype/demo stage. Two outputs in preparation: a scoping review positioning the field (Ding first author; target: CoDesign) and a software paper (Ge first author; target: JOSS).

XR research design for inclusive pedagogy

Authored a full proposal for the ESRC North West Social Science DTP (NWSSDTP) Postdoctoral Fellowship (October 2025 entry) on XR-based music pedagogy for neurodivergent learners, drawing on AR/holographic display technology from Liverpool's Design4Life lab (Dr Hao-Ming Xia's museum and cultural-heritage AR research) — not taken forward at the institutional nomination stage. The participatory co-design and cognitive-science evaluation framework it produced now informs the evaluation design being developed for Syn-Aesthetica.

Education

PhD in Education, University of Glasgow · 01/2019–06/2024

Thesis: Music teachers' perceptions of creativity in the context of twenty-first century Chinese education (viva 27 September 2023). Supervisors: Prof. Oscar Odena, Prof. Kay Livingston.

Qualitative design: 17 semi-structured interviews, 24 lesson observations, critical-incident charting, researcher diary; thematic analysis in NVivo. Produced an original seven-stage model of creativity development in music education. Key finding: conservatoire-trained instrumentalists received formal improvisation training; generalist teachers engaged with it only informally.
